# Courses

Learning materials will be based on reviewed knowledge entries and organized into introductory, thematic, and text-reading modules.

## Planned modules

1. Historical origins of Chinese physiognomy
2. Reading editions and historical sources
3. Traditional terms: San Ting, Wu Guan, and Shi Er Gong
4. Comparing historical physiognomy manuals
5. Evidence, interpretation, and the limits of traditional knowledge

Courses are in development. Published lessons will identify their references, scope, and revision dates.
