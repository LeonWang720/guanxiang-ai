# Research Overview

This section explains the project's scope, source policy, and editorial method.

## Research goals

1. Document the formation, transmission, and editions of historical Chinese physiognomy texts.
2. Build a structured index of terminology and traditional classifications.
3. Present source passages with translation and historical context.
4. Turn reviewed material into accessible learning resources.

## Scope and limits

The project studies how traditional ideas were recorded and transmitted. It does not claim that facial appearance predicts a person's character or life outcomes. Source text, bibliographic facts, later interpretation, modern scholarship, and editorial commentary must remain clearly distinguished.
