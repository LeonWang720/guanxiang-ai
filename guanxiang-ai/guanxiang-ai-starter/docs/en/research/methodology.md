# Methodology & Evidence

## Source layers

- **Primary text:** identify the title, edition, volume, chapter, and page. Mark uncertain details as unverified.
- **Bibliographic records:** record library holdings, publication data, facsimiles, or reliable digitized sources.
- **Modern scholarship:** cite author, publication, year, and page or stable URL.
- **Editorial notes:** label project translations and interpretations clearly.
- **AI-assisted drafts:** treat as drafts requiring human verification.

Never invent quotations, authors, dates, volume numbers, or page references. Do not present historical physiognomic claims as established scientific findings.
