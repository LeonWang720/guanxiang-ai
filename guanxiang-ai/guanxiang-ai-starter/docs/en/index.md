## About the project

**Guanxiang AI · Chinese Physiognomy Knowledge Base** is an open digital cultural research project for readers around the world. It studies the textual history, concepts, historical contexts, and transmission of traditional Chinese physiognomy.

The project separates classical source texts, bibliographic information, modern scholarship, and educational materials so that claims can be traced and reviewed.

## Important note

Traditional physiognomy is studied here as a historical and cultural tradition. Statements found in historical texts are not scientific findings. This project does not infer a person's character, morality, wealth, health, lifespan, relationships, or destiny from facial appearance, nor does it support such inferences in consequential decisions.
