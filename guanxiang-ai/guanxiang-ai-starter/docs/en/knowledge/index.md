# Traditional Knowledge Topics

This section organizes concepts found in historical texts. It is intended to explain terminology and historical context, not to judge real individuals.

## Initial categories

- **Three facial divisions (San Ting):** a traditional vertical division of the face.
- **Five features (Wu Guan):** eyebrows, eyes, nose, mouth, and ears in traditional classification.
- **Twelve palaces (Shi Er Gong):** a system of named facial regions in traditional manuals.
- **Facial-form categories:** descriptions and classifications of facial outlines in historical sources.

Each topic will include source references, textual differences, explanatory diagrams, and modern critical context. Unverified material will be labeled accordingly.
