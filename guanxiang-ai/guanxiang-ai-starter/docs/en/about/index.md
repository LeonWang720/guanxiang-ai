# About Guanxiang AI

## Project

Guanxiang AI · Chinese Physiognomy Knowledge Base is a bilingual digital research and learning project for a global audience. It focuses on the literature, history, and cultural contexts of traditional Chinese physiognomy.

## Access and reuse

The website is publicly readable. Unless a page states otherwise, original project writing, knowledge cards, courses, and illustrations are intended to be released under **CC BY-NC 4.0**. Attribution is required and commercial use is not permitted without prior written permission.

Third-party editions, annotations, translations, images, fonts, and other materials are not automatically covered by this license. Their own rights and license terms apply.

## Research position

The project studies physiognomy as a historical and cultural tradition. It does not claim that facial appearance can scientifically predict character, ability, morality, wealth, health, or destiny. Such inferences should not be used in hiring, admissions, lending, healthcare, or other consequential decisions.
