# Classical Texts

This catalog will document historical texts related to Chinese physiognomy. Inclusion in the research plan does not mean that an author's identity, date of composition, or textual history has already been established.

## Initial research list

- *Ma Yi Shen Xiang* (《麻衣神相》): editions, structure, and transmission require verification.
- *Shen Xiang Quan Bian* (《神相全编》): compare editions and identify the sources of incorporated passages.
- *Liu Zhuang Xiang Fa* (《柳庄相法》): verify attribution, editions, and historical references.
- *Tai Qing Shen Jian* (《太清神鉴》): verify title variants, volumes, editions, and catalog records.
- *Yue Bo Dong Zhong Ji* (《月波洞中记》): examine early textual evidence and comparative materials.

## Citation policy

Every quotation must identify the edition used. Online transcriptions are discovery aids, not verified editions. Modern punctuation, annotations, and translations must be separately attributed, with their rights respected.
